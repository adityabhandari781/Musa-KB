 The worst way to grow old is to imagine what if, what if I did that, what if I pulled it off, what if it actually worked. What ifs are for when you are a young kid, that's when you imagine, what if, imagine if, what if. When you are mature enough to pursue your dreams, there are no what ifs. You create your own reality. And then when you grow old, that's when you can be nostalgic, that's when you can look back with fond memories and look at everything you've accomplished, the failures, the lessons, the wins, everything. But you don't want to grow old and have those same what ifs as when you were a kid. Because when you were a kid, those what ifs have like an innocence to them, because you're kind of waiting for the time to ripen so you can pursue it. But when you're old, there is no time ripening anymore. Those what ifs, those aspirational what ifs turn into regretful what ifs.
