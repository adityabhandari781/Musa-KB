However, if you keep pushing forward with a clear goal in mind, and if you progressively adjust your thinking and actions along the way, you'll eventually calibrate yourself to get the results you want. So an example of this is when I first started kickboxing, I was not calibrated to be a kick boxer or for success. I would flinch. If someone threw a punch at my face, I would flinch, turn my head away. I'm not calibrated. I didn't have a rhythm. I was tense. I never threw my right hand because I was afraid I would get countered if I threw my right hand. So I would stick behind my jab, stay safe behind my jab. I was overall just not calibrated for being a kick boxer. But over time, if you just keep going, keep going, keep going, and you refine your thoughts and your actions, you start getting the rhythm. And now you're calibrated for it. And this applies to any endeavor in life. So if you want to start anything, understand that you are not calibrated for success. You will experience mostly failure.
