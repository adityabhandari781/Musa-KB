One important thing to note as well is that in the beginning you need the most discipline. When you are building from the ground up that's when you need the most discipline because everything and everyone around you is steering you off the path. Your own feelings, people, social media, everything. So you need the most discipline. You also don't see a return on your effort right away. So you need sheer discipline. You need to turn the volume on your inner voice up all the way up. But as you get going, momentum starts to help you, starts to get on your side. And then as you get good at it, it turns into obsession and then it turns into devotion. And as you go through this process you need less and less and less discipline. It goes more smoothly if you will. But in the beginning you got to be a fucking dog about it.
