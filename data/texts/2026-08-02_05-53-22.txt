As a man,
You naturally want to become more capable.

There are predators in this world,

And you don’t want them imposing their will on you,

let alone your loved ones.

That’s why men are drawn to learning how to fight.

How to use weapons.

How to survive.

You want to become unfuckwithable.

But capability begins long before that:

Not oversharing your plans,

Knowing when not to explain yourself.

Mastering your impulses,
Having clear boundaries.

Physical capability is obvious.

Mental and emotional capability aren’t.

But they protect your life just as much.

Your impulsive side attracts predators.

The first man you must become capable of handling…

is yourself.
