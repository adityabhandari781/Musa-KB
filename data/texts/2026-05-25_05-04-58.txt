It’s Monday,

This will be a great week, 
IF you move with aim & execute. 🏹

REMEMBER:

Until thought is linked with purpose,

There is no intelligent accomplishment.

Most people let their thoughts drift, while they scroll social media.

Not understanding,
Aimlessness is a vice.

Pro-tip:

Use a journal more than you use your phone this week.

Think on paper.
Plan on paper.
Reflect on paper.

And do most of your work on a laptop, instead of the phone.

The spirit of your aim answers your prayers. 🏹

-OUT.
