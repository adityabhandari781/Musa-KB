I wish I had a brother that warned me for this,

But the moment you don’t have a vision, you’ll fuck up your life.

Bad habits.
Self-sabotage.
Drifting.
Breaking commitments.

Almost all of them happened after I reached a goal.

Because I had no new mountain to climb.

The idea that you win,
Then relax at the top…

That’s a fantasy.

If you don’t choose a higher mountain,

you’ll start walking downhill,

Just so you can spend years reclimbing that mountain again.

Learn from my mistakes bro,

Always set your aim on a higher mountain.

Never go through life sightless.
