You don’t fail because your goals are too big.

You fail because your system is too weak.

An entrepreneur doing deepwork every morning follows a system.

A rapper in the studio late at night follows a system.

The entrepreneur has one.
The athlete has one.
The artist has one.

Everyone operating at a high level
follows a system.

It doesn’t matter what the system is.
What matters is that you have one.

Goals give direction.
Systems create results.
