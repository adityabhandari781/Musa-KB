You have to become a fan of your own story,

The bum friends,
The wasted time,

All shit you went through..

ALL of it.

It’s all part of the plot.

Imagine you wrote it yourself.
And there’s a glorious ending.

See it.

The main character ends up becoming the biggest G.

Despite everything, he recreated himself..

And turned it all around.

Believe in the ending.

Then let consistent effort
make that ending real.
