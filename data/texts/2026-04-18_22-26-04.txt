Life is like fighting.

If you’re not forcing the pace,
you’re fighting at someone else’s rhythm

The government,
Your environment,
Your family…

You have to FORCE THE PACE.

Raise the standards.

Effort.
Tenacity.
Persistence.

Don’t let life happen to you. 
You must happen to it.

Apply pressure until what you want, DROPS in front of your feet.

Set. The. Pace.
