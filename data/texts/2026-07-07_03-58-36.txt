Suicidal people swear they hate themselves, but they don’t.

Not at their core being.
Our essence is light.

They just hate the current version of themselves.

And that’s ok.
It’s fine to hate the current version of you.

Every 6 months, I hate the current version of me too.

I hate him with every fibre of my being.

But kill him through action, new habits and a new identity.

Not through ending life.
