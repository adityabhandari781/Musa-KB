The danger of half-hearted effort,
is that it often gets rewarded.

People praise you.

You make decent progress.

You can even create an illusion that you gave more than you actually did.

Until one day,

That half-hearted shit costs you your dreams.

Put your whole heart into your work.
Especially when no one is watching.

Or disrespect your dreams.

While regret waits for its time to collect the bill.
