Get problems out of your system and just put it into paper.

Don’t think about solving it, just write everything off of you.

You can’t move freely if you carry too much clutter in body and mind.

It drains your energy,
Just like how a computer with many tabs open slows down.

When you write the clutter onto paper, the clutter is no longer part of you.

You are detached from it. 

Which frees up energy and gives you clarity.
