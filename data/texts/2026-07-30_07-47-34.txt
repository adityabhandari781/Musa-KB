Everyone wants to be a leader, until they see what it takes.

You have to be the hardest worker on the team.

Regardless of how you feel.

You can’t show weakness to your people,
Ever.

Plus you must stay in touch with the sentiment of your people.

So closely…

that you can smell the faintest hint of doubt

while it’s still in its infancy.

Lastly, when you lose, it’s your loss alone. Can’t blame anyone.

But when you win, it’s a collective win.

Leadership is not easy.
And yet you must embrace it.

Sooner or later, wether in business or your family life, you’ll have to be a leader.
