What if it’s all perfect?

What if God only asks you for consistent effort?

What if the pain you feel now,
and the pain you felt back then…

Is all part of the story?

What if you’re not meant to understand how or when it’ll all come full circle…

But the full circle moment is already written?

What if your only job is to keep moving?

Because the circle only becomes complete,
through continuous motion..

I think we are alike you and I.

We both have anger.
Perhaps even tears in the heart.

Channel all of it toward effort.

It’s all a man can do.
