The amount of times I showed up to training,

Running on poor sleep,
But didn’t say a word.

Understand,

Whatever your reason is for being compromised,

The most amateur thing you can do,
is mention it before the session starts.

Indirectly,

You’re asking for mercy before the mission even begins…

Giving yourself an excuse in advance for poor performance.

If you really want to bring it up,
Do it after the session.

But before it starts?

Stay silent.
Focus on the mission.

(This doesn’t just apply to training.)
