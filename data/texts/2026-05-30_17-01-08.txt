Unfriendly Reminder:

Anyone that unfollowed you, yet follows people you know,

Made a bet.

Maybe not consciously.
But deep down they decided:

“He’s never gonna make it.”

Most people will tell you not to take it personally.

But I understand human nature too well to give you that advice.

So I say,

Face Reality:

If they believed you were about to become extraordinary,

they would’ve stayed to watch.

Human nature loves winners.

People like being associated with momentum.

In their eyes,

You’re just another guy who talked big and faded away.

Take note.
