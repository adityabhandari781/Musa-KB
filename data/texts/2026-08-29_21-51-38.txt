Family members will always minimise what you went through.

“It wasn’t that bad.”
“You had a good childhood.”
“I had it worse.”

Just like they will always downplay your achievements.

The truth is,
Familiarity breeds contempt

Your story will never be remarkable to them.

And that’s fine.

But the danger of letting others downplay your past
is that you lose the gift hidden inside your trauma.

Regardless of what they say,
your body knows your pain.

The body keeps the score.

Feel it fully.

Then take that pain
and use it to recreate yourself.

This is your story my friend.
Fuck anyone who downplays it.
