Sometimes I imagine what it’d be like growing up with a father’s guidance.

A dad teaching how to overcome fear, deal with losses, discipline and other life lessons..

But that imagination is short-lived.

I snap out of the fantasy,

Remind myself I have to claim the last name,

Make it mean something.

Have more kids than him.
Be a better father than him,

Make that entire side of the family unworthy of the last name.

People would call me extreme for my mentality..

I don’t give a fuck.
