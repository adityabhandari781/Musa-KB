All my life, I was surrounded by bums.

Playing video games.
Wasting a lot of time.

I believed it didn’t matter.

I was destined to become successful anyways.

Until one day, I saw a flash of my future.

10 years ahead,

Surrounded by the same friends.

Still making the same jokes.
Wasting time.

It was just a flash..
But enough to make me feel fear.

A terrifying future.

That’s when I ruthlessly cut everyone off and raised my standards.

WAKE UP TO REALITY.
