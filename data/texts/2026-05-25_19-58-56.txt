FUEL

It doesn’t matter where your fuel comes from..

I can’t pinpoint exactly what drives me,
Nor do I give a fuck.

Everything must be reframed into power.

And that power must be turned into action. 

That’s all there is to it.

Inner drive is meaningless if you don’t use it to move mountains.

Build something legendary.

When it’s all said and done, 

You can reflect to see what drove you to the point of madness.

Until then, use everything.
