Persuasion technique 1: THE FRAME.

I never say “village”.
Sounds unimportant.

I always reframe as:

“Early infrastructure of a new way of living.”


When you negotiate, 

People think inside the frame you gave them.

If I say “village”, 

He might imagine some village in Somalia.

Now he’s out with 1 foot, before we even started talking.

Always set the right frame immediately.

Reframing late is harder than framing early
