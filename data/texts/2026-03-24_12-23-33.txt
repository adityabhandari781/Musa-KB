If you’re a risk taker,
you will take losses.

That’s part of the game.

Most guys let a temporary loss defeat them.

They replay it over and over
and become overly cautious.

They don’t understand:

The real defeat
is becoming gun-shy ⚠️

Like a fighter who gets knocked out once & loses all his aggression.

Or a kid who falls off his bike
and never rides again.

True defeat is becoming hesitant.

Never beat yourself up over losses.
Reflect, learn & move forward.
