Calm in hell:

When everything breaks down around you,

Don't look for escapism or distractions..

Pain is the realest feeling that exists

Tell yourself

"I belong in this hell."

Strip away every distraction until there is nothing but silence.

Then sit in that pain and suffering.

That’s where you see things clear:

No one is coming to save you.

You see those who wronged you.

You see how you wronged yourself.

There’s no bullshit down here.
It's all painful.

But as you go deeper and deeper in that hell,

Something strange happens.

You reach a calm state...
Almost Zen-like. 

Pain no longer feels like an enemy.
You don’t care about pain anymore..

You even welcome it..
You enjoy pain.

This is the version of you, you didn’t even know exists.

Most people will never awaken it, 

Because they always look for escapism, comfort or distractions.

Very few people voluntarily visit the deepest layers of hell.

Wake up to reality..
