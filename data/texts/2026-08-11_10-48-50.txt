As you rise in wealth, 

You go from the broke matrix, to the money matrix.

Where you'll see everyone copying each-other at mastermind events.

The way they talk, think, dress.

Their only personality is business, running ads, funnels, AI automation.

I remind myself of two things:

1. Stay humble, you can still learn from these guys, boring as they may be.

2. At any level, never forsake yourself. Never become one of them


PS. Notice how the people that make it to the big leagues are always unique.

They are never carbon copies of someone else.
