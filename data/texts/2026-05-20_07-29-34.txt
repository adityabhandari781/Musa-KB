🚨TODAY’s CHALLENGE:

✨ Gratitude prayer.

1. Thank God for anything you’re grateful for.

Anything that comes to mind.
It doesn’t matter what it is.

Repeat that gratitude over and over,
until you feel it opening your heart.

It’s a warm feeling.

Only then move to the next thing you’re grateful for.

Again,
repeat it until you feel the same shift.

Last but not least,

After expressing gratitude for what already exists in the present moment,

2. Do the same for your future.

Your vision.
Your goals.
Your future family.
Your success.

See it as already realized.

Repeat gratitude until your heart opens up.

That state is unbeatable.

All fomo, fear and low frequency will vanish.

Because gratitude moves you into the emotional state of your future self.


PS. Don’t rush this process.

You know you’ve done it right, when after you finish prayer.

You have a calm certainty.
