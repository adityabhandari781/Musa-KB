There is no need to be miserable.

Even if you didn’t hit your goal yet.

In your mind you must unlock the sensation of it already being reality.

For example:

In my mind,
I am a father of 8-10 kids.  

There is no need for me to stress about them not being here yet.

It’s a matter of time.

The delay gives me time to improve as a leader, as a father, grow wiser, accomplish more

Etc 

Being miserable is never the answer.
Things are perfect.
