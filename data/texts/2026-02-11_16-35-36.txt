I AM:

I always tell my people,
Anything you say after “I am..”

Is going to be on your gravestone.

So If you say:

“I am tired.”
“I am a gamer”
“I am depressed.”
“I am an introvert.”

Imagine your gravestone saying:

“Here lies…

He was a tired, depressed & introverted gamer.”

That’s a pathetic gravestone.

I tell myself:

“I am an entrepreneur.”
“I am a high energy person.”

“I am a creative”

“I am a physical specimen.”
“I am a strategist”

UNDERSTAND:

Anything you say or even THINK after “I AM” = your identity.

Pick your words after “I AM” very carefully.
