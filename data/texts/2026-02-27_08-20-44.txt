When you’re just starting, don’t expect allies.

No one gives a shit about you or your goals.

You’re alone on the battlefield.

And yeah, it’s lonely..

But there’s someone watching from the shadows.
Her name is Momentum.

Momentum is like a lover.
She gets turned on by persistence.

She remembers every rep, every day you showed up, every time you kept going when nobody was watching.

Days will pass where it feels like nothing is working.

But the small actions you take eventually compound,

And before you realize it,

Your persistent spirit seduces Momentum.

And with Her on your side, nothing can stop you.

Remember this the next time you feel like skipping the daily work:

Momentum is always watching.

She wants to love you.
But can’t if you lack consistency.

The world is full of such men.

Show her persistence,
Daily.

And sooner or later..she melts.

Wake up to reality..
