Reminder:

Everything in this life is a test.
The Creator created this beautifully.

Every hardship carries a lesson.

We know this to be true in video games, but tend to forget about it in reality.

You can even look at earthly materials and see how this applies.

Example:

Diamonds and Coal are made of the same element (carbon)

The only difference is
The pressure they’ve endured.

Embrace hardship. Find the lesson,

And you evolve a step closer to the higher version of yourself.
