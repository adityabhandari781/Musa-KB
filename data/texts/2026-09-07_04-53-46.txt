DEALING WITH REGRET:

The best way to deal with regret is atonement.

People always say,

“Just let it go.”

But that’s bad advice.

If regret was meant to be immediately discarded,

it would serve no purpose.

Letting go comes after you’ve made up for it.

Make an oath to yourself.
Change the way you move forward.

Atone.

Then let go and forgive yourself.
