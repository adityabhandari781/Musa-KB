I DO THIS FOR UNDERDOGS:

The value I’m about to give on my YouTube channel is for underdogs.

I can’t relate to people who had guidance.

Who were set up for success.
Who had a someone show them the way from day one.

Must be nice.

All I know is being written off.
Being a “good-for-nothing” kid.

I had to force myself into success.

That’s why I don’t give a fuck about hate.

No one gave a fuck about me back then.
So their opinions now mean nothing.

I don’t speak to the chosen ones.

I speak to those who were destined for nothing.

The overlooked.
The underestimated.
The ones no one bet on.

I see myself in underdogs.
And everything I’m about to do
is for them.
