People pay attention to class.
Especially at higher levels.

If your behavior lacks class,
don’t assume people don’t notice.

They do.

Bums overlook it.
High-caliber people don’t.

If they text you for something,
and you take hours/days to reply…

Note is taken.

If you show up late,
expecting an apology to justify it..

Note is taken.

If someone shows you generosity,
and you don’t reciprocate…

Note is taken.

People who operate at a high level know:

Character is revealed in the smallest patterns.

You can get away with being classless, only when surrounded by low lives.

At higher levels, they take note of your character.

Class is a virtue.
