For most men,

Our warrior side is born from pain.
Being powerless.

It protects the wounded inner child.
And says “never again”

The problem is, 
Most guys become only the warrior.

The warrior protects the inner child.
But he can also bury him.

And inside that inner child are often your greatest gifts:

Curiosity.
Creativity.
Wonder.
Play.

Ironically, those gifts are often what create the life you’re actually chasing.

Don’t get me wrong,

There are plenty of successful men operating in full warrior mode.

Goggins, Tate, Hormozi.

But note how they also seem to have miserable tendencies.

Not because being a warrior makes you miserable, but like there’s something missing..

Something they’re denying themselves.

Then you have someone like Joe Rogan or even a Chris Williamson,

Who do have that curious child and playfulness still alive.

Most guys go
Wounded Child → Warrior

But what if the highest level is
Wounded Child → Warrior → Integrated Man

Just food for thought ;)
