I know many guys with this mindset:

“Without supplements, you’re in trouble.”

Nothing against supplements.
Can be great.

But if you think you need them to function,
You’re falling for marketing.

The same way people were sold:

“Breakfast is the most important meal of the day.”

👆That saying was advertisement from the cereal and bacon industry in the 1900s. 

No different from
“Everyone needs therapy”

Pushed by the mental health industry.


Anytime a paid solution is presented as something you NEED,

question it.

Often it’s marketing dressed up as necessity.
