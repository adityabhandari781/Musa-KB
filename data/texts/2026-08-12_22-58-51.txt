Sometimes I think of Muhammed Ali.

The man had a fierce spirit.

Even when he had Parkinson disease, Brain damage,

Was a shell of himself and could hardly move his body.

He still had a fierce spirit.
You could see it in his eyes.

It just makes me wonder..

In an afterlife,

Do those who carried a fierce spirit through this life
remain more fierce, more alive,

than those who moved through this world
like dimmed lights?

The body dies.

But the intensity of the spirit
is something death cannot take.
