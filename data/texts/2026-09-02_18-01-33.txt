Stop looking for your purpose.

Only an idiot does that.

Purpose isn’t hiding somewhere waiting to be discovered.

You create purpose by living purposefully.

A purposeful man, you must be.

Take responsibility,
Pursue difficult things,
Serve something beyond yourself.
Create.
Love.
Build.

Do these things long enough,
and you’ll stop asking:

“What is my purpose?”

The path illuminates itself as you walk it.
