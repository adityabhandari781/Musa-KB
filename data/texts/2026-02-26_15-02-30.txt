THE REALITY OF LABELS:

Novices say things like 
“I am tired” or “I am unlucky”.

Nerfing their own reality with these labels.

And you might think you’re smart..
You might use “empowering” labels,

But here’s what most people fail to understand:

Even empowering labels have weaknesses.. 

Think about it,

“I am intelligent”
Can make you avoid situations where you could look stupid.

“I am spiritual”
Can make you avoid ambition or material responsibility.

“I am an entrepreneur” 
Can make you avoid work that feels operational.

Understand:

Labels have purpose.
They can empower you,

But they also narrow your identity,  

This limits you and puts an invisible cage around you.

The key is this:

Use labels when they strengthen you.

But drop them the second they don’t serve you.
Become Formless in an instant.

And if you must keep only one label, let it be this:

Learner.

“I am a learner” has no limits.
There is no cage.

A learner is never finished.
A learner assumes evolution.

Wake up to reality.
