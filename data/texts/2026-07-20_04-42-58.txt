THE LOOP:

Life will keep you in the same loop

Until you learn the lesson,
connect the dots,
and break the pattern.

This applies to everything.

Health.
Wealth.
Relationships.

Think about it.

Those who can’t build a great physique assume it’s steroids.

Those who can’t build wealth assume it’s a scam.

Those who fail with women assume all women are hoes.

But can you blame them?

Imagine being trapped in a maze,
watching other people walk freely.

The cope is frustration.

It helps them explain away why they’re still stuck.

Wherever you’re stuck,

there’s a puzzle piece you haven’t found yet. 🧩

Find it.
Break the loop.

Wake up to reality..
