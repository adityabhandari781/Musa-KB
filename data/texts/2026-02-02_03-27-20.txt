When I was 9 years old, I learned a valuable life lesson:

Hesitation gets you killed.

I remember riding my bike in a bad neighbourhood,

When a Moroccan guy said: “why are you looking at me?”

This was a bad neighbourhood and I was scared, so I said nothing. 

But then he walked towards me and shouted louder.

“What are you looking at?!”

He got close and said 

“that’s my bike. You stole it, Step off.”

“STEP OFF BEFORE I BREAK YOU.”

My brother came driving on his bike behind me, 

Jumped off his bike, and sprinted towards him. 

I saw instant fear in the guy and he ran away. While yelling “its a joke”

My brother was very aggressive at this time.
He ran after him like he wanted to kill him.

In that moment I learned:

People’s spirit grow when you hesitate.

The perceived power advantage turns them into a blood thirsty hyena.

Never hesitate, always act bold.

This applies in confrontation, business, sparring,
Negotiation.. in LIFE.

If in the first encounter you go on the defensive,

You bring out the Hyenas.
HYENAS CIRCLE HESITANT PREY.
