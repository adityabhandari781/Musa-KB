Destructive guilt:

“I’m a bad person.”
“I’m a piece of shit”
“I don’t deserve…”

Productive guilt:

“I know I’m capable of more.”
“I betrayed my standards.”
“I abandoned my mission.”

Destructive instills shame in your identity.

Productive attacks behavior.

If you feel guilt, draw out the lesson and move forward a better man.

Don’t chain yourself to shame.
