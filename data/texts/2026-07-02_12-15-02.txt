I don’t feel threatened by any man who doesn’t live true to himself.

True courage is becoming your own man.

Since the day you were born,

the world has been telling you who to be.

How to behave.

What to strive for.

What to believe.

Every philosophy claims to have the answer.

But the only question that matters is:

What is true to you?

How could I fear a man

who abandoned himself
just to be accepted by the crowd?

Never.
