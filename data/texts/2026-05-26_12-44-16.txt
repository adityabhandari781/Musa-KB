I am never satisfied.

Broke people see that as unhealthy.

But every artist I know is dissatisfied.
Every athlete.
Every entrepreneur.

Even my 3-year-old daughter,
who does 100 burpees, ice baths, and the splits,

STILL talks about growing stronger.

Dissatisfaction is a creative state.
The human spirit is made to evolve.

The idea that you accomplish something,

And stay satisfied forever…

Is an illusion.

Satisfaction is the neighbour of death.
