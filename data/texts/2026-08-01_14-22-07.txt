Fiction is often more powerful than facts.

We live in stories.

Dragon Ball Z has inspired more young men to train…

than countless scientific studies on the benefits of exercise.

Why?

Because people don’t just remember facts.

They become emotionally invested in stories.

They want to become the hero.

That’s why myths, movies and novels have shaped civilizations for thousands of years.

Case in point:

When Andrew Tate is in jail,
His X account always grows Exponentially 

Because he applies fictional storytelling.

He doesn’t have phone,
But a ghostwriter tweets for him,

No one cares that it isn’t factually Tate who’s writing.

The compelling writing and compelling stories move people.

Once you understand the power of stories,

you stop communicating information.

You start moving people.
