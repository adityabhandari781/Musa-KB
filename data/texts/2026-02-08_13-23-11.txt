UPGRADE YOUR STATS:

What you say is nullified or amplified by who you are.

This is why students mock teachers.
Or some kids roll their eyes at their parents.

These older people may have “wise” ideas,
but have no impressive feats.

So their words carry little weight.

The opposite is also true,

People who’ve accomplished something

(Like rappers or athletes)

Say the most basic shit imaginable
and get quoted like prophets.

You may know the same things,
but your words don’t carry the same weight.

Understand,

Your wisdom is unquotable. Even if you read 9000 books.

Your origin story is irrelevant.
No one cares what you’ve been through.

Plenty of men have suffered less than you,
yet people want to hear them speak.

Level up your stats.
Become the main character..

And every word you speak becomes 100x heavier.

Learn life.
