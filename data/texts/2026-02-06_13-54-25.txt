RUN WILD:

When you grow old
 
Life will FORCE you to slow down.
Your vitality won’t be the same.
 
You’ll lose your intensity.
Your flame will dim.

And in that slowed down time,

All the entertainment you need is your own life story.

That’s when it becomes fun to reminisce. 

So,
 
While you still can,
 
Run wild, 
Take risks,
Make mistakes,
Get humiliated..
Flirt with both victory and defeat.

Remain fierce at all times.
 
You can slow down and be boring later.
 
When you’re young, you’re allowed to be stupid.
 
As long as:
 
- you don’t go to jail (at least not for long)
 
- you don’t die
 
- you don’t permanently damage your health
 
EVERYTHING IS PERMITTED.
 
So Run wild.
Don’t fear defeat.

You are writing the life story that’ll one day inspire your kids.
