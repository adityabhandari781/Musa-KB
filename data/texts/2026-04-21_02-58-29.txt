Did you know:

Tigers Rarely attack humans.

They avoid species they don’t see as prey.

Humans sit at the top of the food chain.

Yet most live spiritually and physiologically like prey.

Afraid to go after their goals,
Waiting for permission,

Fear of judgement..

If a tiger saw how timid you live, it would regret ever fearing us.

You are the apex predator.

The programming made you forget your own nature.

Take what you want.
