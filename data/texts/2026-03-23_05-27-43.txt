Ever seen someone do affirmations?

A grown man talking to himself in the mirror.

Looks ridiculous, right?

FOOL,

We ALL use affirmations.

To affirm is to make something firm in your mind.

Anything you say OR THINK after “I am”
is an affirmation.

“I’m inconsistent.”
“I am bad with technology.”
“I always procrastinate.”

You’ve been programming yourself all along, unconsciously.

Time to do it intentionally.

Affirmations aren’t about standing in front of a mirror,

If that feels off, don’t do that.

Just write them down.
15 times. Daily.

And if they feel like lies,
Keep them grounded:

“I follow through on what I start.”
“I execute daily, regardless of mood.”

That’s how you rebuild your self-image.

You’ve been programming yourself negatively.

If you have an ounce of respect for yourself,
do the opposite. ⛓️‍💥❤️
