The Gift of Authenticity:

The gift of authenticity isn’t just money.

Plenty of fake individuals get rich.

The real gift of authenticity is that you attract your tribe.

In the long run, that brings money, opportunities, fulfillment, and relationships that energize you instead of drain you.

But here’s where most guys struggle:

When you move with the courage to be your true self,

You don’t get rewarded.
You get punished first.

You don’t attract light. You attract darkness first.

First, you repel the people who thought they knew you.

Then you'll attract judgment.

And because authenticity requires vulnerability, your character gets attacked from every angle.

Sometimes behind your back.

Sometimes by the people you thought were friends or family.

Only after weathering that storm do your tribe of people begin to find you.

Most guys don’t have the spine to stand tall in that storm. 🌪️

But by avoiding the storm, they pay the ultimate price:

Never living a life that’s true to themselves.
