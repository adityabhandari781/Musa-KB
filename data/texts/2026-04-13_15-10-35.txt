There is no such thing as “procrastination.”

There’s only Your old identity solving a problem.

The problem being:

identity death.

It delays, distracts..
Negotiates with you.

Because the old self knows
consistent action will kill it.

That’s why you avoid the work.


The moment you use the label “procrastination,”
you give flesh to an illusion.

You make it feel like a real thing.

Remove the term.

And what remains is simple:

resistance to becoming someone new.
