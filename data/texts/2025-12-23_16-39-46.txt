Take pride in your work.

There’s a strong correlation between people who obsess over their craft
And those who become successful.

We live in an outcome-obsessed era.

Everyone wants the end result.
Very few respect the process.

Almost no one truly studies & honors their craft.

No matter how locked in I was during my Ghost mode..

No matter how angry or driven I was to make money.

I always understood one thing:

Those who don’t respect work, do sloppy work.
And sloppy work brings no value to the marketplace.
