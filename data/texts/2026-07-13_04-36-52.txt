There is a version of you that’s a soldier.

There is a version of you that’s a pro athlete.

These versions of you aren’t different species.

They’ve just accepted what their mission demands of them.

You may not be fighting a war.

You may not be competing for a world title.

But if you have a dream,

why do you treat your mission with any less seriousness?

Be militant with your priorities.

Take care of yourself like a professional athlete.

Your mission may be different.
But the level of standards shouldn’t be.

Respect your mission.
