The most important thing you can do right now
is stop following the script:

📝 Hang around people
you only know from proximity.

📝 Convince yourself they are real friends.

📝 Tell yourself you’re trying…
while you scroll for hours a day.

SPOILER ALERT:

That story doesn’t end well.

The main character ends up old & miserable.

Bitter towards everyone.

Haunted by the ghosts of untapped potential who remind him:

“You could’ve done it in your Golden Years. 

Why didn’t you..? Why you piece of shit.”

Listen,

It’s not too late to grab the pen.

You must author your life.

Your lack of direction
is why you watch others.

And deep down,
you know it.

You are the author.
Pick up the pen.

Turn the phone off.

Write it down:

What do you actually want?

No limits from your environment, family or religion.

No programmed beliefs of what is possible and what isn’t.

What do YOU want?

What is your desired reality?

Don’t rush this process.
Let it flow.

From mind to paper.

Once you know where you’re going,

Your life starts writing itself.

SPOILER ALERT:

Your story is magnificent.
And inspires many.
