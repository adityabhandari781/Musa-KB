Sit with God.

Ask Him:

“How can I make myself proud before the year’s over?”

I asked myself that question a long time ago,

Around this same time of year.

I had much regret over wasted time & potential.

My wife didn’t understand.
I was a hustler. And it looked like I was doing good, from the outside.

But she didn’t know I promised myself to built a legitimate business.

Another broken promise to myself..
I hadn’t build shit.

I was pissed,

couldn’t see a way to make legitimate income online.

Certainly not in 3-4 months time.

But then I asked

 “How can I be proud of myself before the year ends?”

And that question changed everything.

It showed me a way.
I committed to it.

And it changed my life quicker than I could’ve imagined.

To this day,

That was one of the best feelings I’ve ever experienced.

There is a way.

It will require 100% commitment.
And you won’t see the full path.

But sit with God, ask how you can make yourself proud.

And you will see the first steps.

You’ll see what to eliminate from your life also.

That’s all you need.
