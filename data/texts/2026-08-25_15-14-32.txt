There’s nothing to talk about when I’m regaining my strength.

I have no interest in reminiscing on how strong I used to be, before a setback.

I never operated like that.

I’ve always been focused on getting back after it.

If I got weaker?

There is nothing to talk about.

No one I want to talk to.
Not my friends.

No one.

“We’ll see.”

That’s the only thought in my mind.

A battle against myself
in its purest form.

A body that decided to sacrifice muscle and strength.

And a spirit that will punish the body for that decision.

They’ll see me again when I’ve conquered myself.

Until then,
there is nothing to talk about.
