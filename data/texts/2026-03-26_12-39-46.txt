Meditation Technique: FEEL PAIN.

I’ll teach you a technique to break your limiting beliefs:

Think of 2 beliefs
that have been holding you back.

Examples:

“I’m not good with technical things.”

“I can’t speak on camera.”

“Making money online is complicated.”

“I can’t move out of my country”

“Cant find a good woman”

Close your eyes. 

Take a deep breath in…
slow exhale.

Pick 2 limiting beliefs you have.
Feel them.

What have they cost you?

Your money.
Progress?

Feel the weight of it.

NOW, Pull yourself 5 years into the future.

Same beliefs.

You’re dragging them like a weight.

A snowball that only got bigger.

Look at your life.

What did it cost you?

Feel it.

NOW go 10 years ahead.

That weight became even bigger🪨

What opportunities did you miss?

How do you see yourself now?

Feel it.

Now 20 years forward…

Opportunities have passed.

What would you say to yourself?

Are you proud?

Would you cope?

Sit with that.

Now come back.

None of that happened.

Relief. 😌

Shake your body out. 

Take a breath.

Change the belief.
Change the story. ✍️

Your life is still in your hands.
