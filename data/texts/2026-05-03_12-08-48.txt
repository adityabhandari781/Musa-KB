CHAIN YOUR HANDS:

The world chained your mind,
and freed your impulses.

This is the greatest Genjutsu of all.

You must reverse the order.

Unchain your mind.
Chain your hands.

Most men think in limitations,
while acting without restraint.

Scroll social media all day.

Watch porn.

Eat like a pig..

The paradox:

The more you restrict your hands,
The more powerful you become.
