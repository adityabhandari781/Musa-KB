THE BIGGER PREDATOR:

I used to be afraid of my own brother.

He’d beat me up and punch me in the face when we were still kids.

I was a kind hearted kid.
And expected the world to be fair.

I expected someone to help me.

How stupid I was.

You can’t negotiate for peace if you’re powerless.

Not when you’re dealing with a bully.

Not in business.

And not on a world wide scale.

Peace requires power.

The idea that others won’t fuck with you because you’re “good” is a fallacy.

This brutal world is full of sharks.

The only way to deal with a shark is to be a killer whale.

To be FAR FROM WEAK.
