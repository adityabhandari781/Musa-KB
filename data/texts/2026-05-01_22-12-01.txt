Whatever you try to avoid in life,

Will hit you in the face after some time.

So you can lie to yourself that money is not important and that you can live in poverty,

But only until your mother gets sick.

Only until you hit your car or until you don’t have money to take your girl out to eat.

You need money. You know it.

So start that business.
Be more ruthless about your ambition.

Coping gets you hurt in the long run.
