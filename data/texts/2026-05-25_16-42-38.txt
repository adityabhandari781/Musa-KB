I want my child to surpass me in every possible way.

Smarter. Stronger. Higher level entrepreneur.

More professional. 
More class.

A bigger risk taker.
A better speaker.

But there’s no point in my kids surpassing me if the bar is set low.

Therefore,

I am sharpening myself In all areas.
Constantly dissatisfied.

They should conquer a demon to surpass me.

Nothing more disgusting than a father that’s easily surpassed.
