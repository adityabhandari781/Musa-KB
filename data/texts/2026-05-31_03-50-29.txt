The devil moves in unseen ways.

That friend that’s all talk?
Devil.

He’s wasting your time.

Energy vampires? Devil.
They drain your focus.

The person who flatters you is a devil too.

Because flattery breeds comfort.

Most guys are amateurs.
They take words at face value.

But the devil is a liar.

Learn to sense the energy behind words.

Judge words by their effect on you, not how pleasant they sound.
