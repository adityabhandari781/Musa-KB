Life becomes 1000x more enjoyable when you play it like a video game. 

When you recognize that you have the free will to do anything you want.

Step outside of all your limiting beliefs and realize that the desires you have,

Are quests.

You can pick and choose and turn it all into reality.

The ONLY challenge in this game are emotions.

Your character freezes because of emotions.

One day you don’t feel like it.

The other day fear is stopping you.

Another day limiting beliefs stop you..

High Level players use reframes to deal with emotions and stay in control.

Once you do that, you can become the person you always wanted to be.
