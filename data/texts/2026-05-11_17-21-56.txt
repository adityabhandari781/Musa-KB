Did you know:

A Rocket burns 90% of its fuel just to escape Earth’s gravity?

It’s violent,
Highly inefficient,

But necessary.

Escaping gravity requires an enormous amount of force.

Personal Transformation is no different.

You must break free from the gravity of your old identity.

That gravity will always keep pulling you back.

Your world will never change..

Unless, you have fuel powerful enough to rupture it:

Pain

Most people try to change their lives with surface-level motivation,

Then wonder why they keep failing.

Fuck that.

Go where it hurts.

Look at the inner child that got pushed aside.

Or revisit the places where you were made to feel worthless, rejected,

Powerless.

Pick whichever hurts the most.

There is enormous energy trapped inside pain.

Most people spend their lives suppressing it.

The few who have to courage to confront it…

Launch.
