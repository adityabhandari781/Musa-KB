Life will throw unexpected problems at you.

Yesterday, my bank asked me to verify all my payments.

Otherwise, they’d freeze my bank account in a few days time.

First thing I did was move the money to a diff bank account and think:

“Fuck that bureaucratic bullshit.”

But then I used a technique I call Reframing Reality.

The key is to reframe the situation from:

“Annoying BS, Why me??”

Reframe:
This is a puzzle that has a solution.

When I play chess, no matter how difficult of a position I am in,

The game is still fun.

if you ever played a challenging game, you know it’s kinda exciting.

By gamifying my bank situation,

I can’t wait to make my counter move.

Overload them more documents than they asked for, 

Give them extra work to do.

Write a professional letter with chatgpt which presents me as a lawyer.

Cover all blindspots, give them headache

And put them in check mate.

Whenever you face problems, reframe it by gamifying it.

Same situation:

Annoying
Exciting
